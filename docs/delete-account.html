Delete your account

Last updated 22 August 2026

You can delete your InnSelf account and its data in either of two ways — you don't need the app installed to start the process.

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

Open InnSelf, go to Profile → Delete account, and confirm. This is the fastest way and takes effect immediately.

Option 2 — By email (no app needed)

Email innself.vvc@gmail.com from the same address your InnSelf account is registered with, with the subject "Delete my InnSelf account". Include your registered phone number so we can confirm we're deleting the right account. We'll process your request within 30 days and confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

What happens next depends on whether your account has any transaction history:

No completed jobs or payments

Your account and everything tied to it — profile, phone number, address, ID documents, messages, job posts and bids — is permanently and completely deleted.

Has completed jobs or payments

Your personal details (name, phone number, email, ID documents) are permanently removed and replaced with an anonymized placeholder. The underlying job, payment, and review records are kept, with your identity stripped out, because the other party in that transaction has a legitimate right to keep their own record of it — the same way a receipt survives even after the store that issued it closes. Nobody can identify you from what remains.

What isn't affected

Deleting your account doesn't reverse a payment that already went through, or delete a review someone else left about a completed job — reviews are the other party's record of their own experience, not yours to withdraw.
