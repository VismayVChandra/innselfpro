What InnSelf is

InnSelf is a marketplace that connects customers who need home services (electrical, plumbing, carpentry, and similar work) with independent local technicians. InnSelf is not itself a service provider. Technicians are independent — not InnSelf employees, agents, or contractors — and are responsible for the quality, safety, and legality of the work they perform. InnSelf's role is to help the two sides find each other, coordinate, and pay.

Who can use InnSelf

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • You must provide accurate information when creating your account and keep it up to date.
  • Technicians must provide a genuine, valid government ID for verification. Submitting false identity documents is grounds for immediate account termination.
  • You're responsible for keeping your account credentials secure. Let us know immediately if you suspect unauthorized access.

Posting and bidding on jobs

  • Customers post job requests describing real work they need done. Technicians submit bids in good faith.
  • Accepting a bid forms an agreement between the customer and the technician — InnSelf is not a party to that agreement and doesn't guarantee the work will meet any particular standard.
  • Either side may cancel before work begins, or reschedule, using the in-app option — please give a real reason, since the other party is notified and repeated late cancellations are visible on your profile.
  • Once work has started or finished, cancellation is no longer available — use the dispute flag if something went wrong instead.

Payments

  • Online payments are processed by Razorpay. InnSelf never sees or stores your card, UPI, or bank details.
  • Cash payments settled in person can be marked as paid in the app by the customer.
  • Payment is expected once a job is marked complete. If you dispute whether work was actually finished to a reasonable standard, use the in-app dispute flag before paying rather than after.
